About Kudenga Tech Lab

Kudenga Tech Lab was established from a simple realization: engineering truly comes alive when theory meets practical experience.

The moment I interacted with real hardware, engineering became more than something to study. It became something to explore, build, test, and improve.

That experience inspired me to create a platform where students and aspiring innovators can access technical guidance, explore engineering projects, and develop their ideas into practical systems.

Our focus spans robotics, electronics, intelligent systems, engineering design, simulation, and technical research. Beyond showcasing innovations, Kudenga Tech Lab aims to contribute to Zambia's technological development by encouraging practical learning, creativity, and problem-solving among young innovators.

Our Mission

Empowering students and aspiring innovators through practical engineering, technical knowledge, and innovation.

Long-term Vision

Contributing to Zambia's technological development by nurturing practical skills and homegrown engineering solutions.
